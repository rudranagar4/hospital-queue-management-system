Dual-Queue Architecture

How the Dual-Queue System Operates

Two independent data structures orchestrated with fairness logic.

Emergency Priority Queue

Data Structure: heapq (Min-Heap)

Emergency cases are stored as (severity, arrival_timestamp, token_id). Severity 1 (Critical) immediately jumps ahead of Severity 2 (Serious) and Severity 3 (Urgent). Equal severity ties are broken chronologically by arrival time.

  • Always served before standard normal cases
  • Automatic re-balancing in O(log N) complexity
  • Real-time severity-based insertion

Normal Routine Queue

Data Structure: collections.deque (FIFO)

Routine consultations are scheduled strictly in First-In, First-Out sequence with fast O(1) appending and popping. Protected against starvation by the {{ max_consecutive_emergency }}:1 fairness rule so normal patients are never permanently postponed.

  • Transparent live waiting time estimation
  • Fair service guaranteed even under peak trauma surges
  • O(1) enqueue and dequeue operations
